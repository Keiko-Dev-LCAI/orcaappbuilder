🔢 Your Video ID — where to find it

Every upload gets a permanent on-chain ID like v3-19. Save it — you need the number only (e.g. 19) if a video ever needs repair.

1
Right after upload — the success screen shows v3-XX in large text. Tap Copy Video ID or Copy share link before closing.
2
My Channel (nav, wallet connected) — each of your videos has a gold ID v3-XX badge. Tap it to copy the number.
3
Open your video — a gold box under the title shows your ID with copy buttons.
4
From a share link — ?video=19&cv=v3 means your ID is 19 (the number in the URL).
5
If playback fails — connect wallet, open your video, tap Fix Now, pick the same original file. Your Video ID is filled in automatically — you never type it.
