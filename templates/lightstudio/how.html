A quick tour of what you can make here, and what it costs. No sign-up — just connect a wallet when you’re ready to pay.

The basics

There is no account and no email. You connect a wallet, look at a free preview, and pay a small amount in LCAI only if you want the clean file.

Every paid creation starts with a free watermarked preview. If you like it, you pay to unlock the watermark-free version. If you don’t, you owe nothing.

Image

Type what you want to see and get a picture. Standard is the everyday option. Premium is the higher-quality pass when you want extra polish.

Standard $0.10 Premium $1.00

Edit

Upload a photo and describe the change — a new background, a different mood, keep the person, and so on. You get an edited picture back.

Video

Type a scene and get a short video. Standard is the everyday clip. Premium is the higher-quality pass, and that’s the one that can include sound.

Standard $0.20 Premium $2.00

Animate

Upload a still photo and it comes to life as a short 5-second clip. Handy for stickers, posts, or a little motion on a portrait.

$0.20

Voice

Type the words and hear them spoken. You pick the language, the speaker, a feeling (happy, sad, angry, or calm), and how fast they talk.

Each speaker is labeled name · who they sound like · accent · style. The name is just a label — pick by how it sounds.

UGC means a casual, conversational voice, like a person talking on their phone. Advertisement means a polished commercial read, like a radio or TV spot.

$0.05

Song

Type a topic and get full lyrics for free. You can edit them. When you’re happy, pay once to turn them into a real sung track.

Choose up to three genres, a length (from a short 6-second clip up to 3:30), a language, and optionally an artist or band style.

Lyrics free Sung track $0.06

Combine

Lay a song or a spoken clip onto a video. This step is free — you already paid for both pieces. Do both in the same visit.

  1. Make a video first. Use the Video tab (or Animate), generate the preview, and pay to unlock it.
  2. Then make a song or a voice clip. Song tab for music, Voice tab for spoken words. Generate, then pay to unlock.
  3. Tap “Add audio to video.” The free button appears once both are unlocked, and your song or voice is laid onto the video.
