About

OrcaMint is the first truly on-chain NFT platform on Lightchain. Your artwork is stored directly on the blockchain — no IPFS, no cloud storage, no servers that can disappear. Every other NFT platform gives you a receipt pointing to a server. OrcaMint puts the actual art on-chain, permanently. Mint with ERC-721 + ERC-2981 royalties. Powered by Lightchain AIVM.
