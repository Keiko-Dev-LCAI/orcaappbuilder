🔭 Vision

Music belongs to creators, not corporations. LightTunes gives musicians and artists a permanent home for their work — one that no company can take away. Built on Lightchain AI, powered by the community. No algorithm. No middleman. Forever.

🚀 How Uploading Works
  1. Connect wallet — tap Connect in the top bar.
  2. Upload — pick your audio file, song title, genre, and optional cover art.
  3. Sign — sign once (no upload fee during the KEIKO promo). The relay stores your song on-chain (usually 1–3 minutes).
  4. Keep the tab open until the progress bar finishes, or use “Browse while uploading.”

Once on-chain, uploads cannot be deleted — only hidden from this app. During the KEIKO promo there is no upload fee (gas in LCAI still applies).

💿 Albums & Collections

An album is a group of songs that share the same Album / Collection name. They appear together on the Albums tab, on your channel, and in Explore.

Create a new album (when uploading)

  1. Fill in Album / Collection (e.g. Summer Vibes EP).
  2. Set Track # — use 1 for the first song, 2 for the next, and so on.
  3. Optional: pick an Album Cover image (shown on the Albums browse page).
  4. Upload each track separately with the same album name and the next track number.

Move a single into an album (already uploaded)

  1. Go to My Songs or your channel — songs without an album show under 🎵 Singles.
  2. Tap ✏️ Edit on the song.
  3. Enter Album / Collection and Track #.
  4. Tap 💾 Save to Blockchain and confirm in your wallet.

Change or add an album cover later

  1. Open the Albums tab → click your album.
  2. Tap 🖼️ Album Cover (only you see this on your own albums).
  3. Pick a square image and sign in your wallet.
💡 Song cover = art for one track. Album cover = art for the whole release on the Albums page.
🔞 Explicit Content

Songs marked explicit are hidden by default for all listeners. They only appear when someone checks 🔞 Show Explicit in the nav bar (their choice, on their device only).

  1. When uploading, check 🔞 Explicit Content if the song has adult language or themes.
  2. When editing, you can change the explicit flag the same way.
  3. Listeners who want explicit music must opt in — it is never shown to everyone automatically.
⚠️ You must mark explicit content honestly. Unmarked explicit songs may be removed without refund.
🧭 Browsing LightTunes
  • All Songs — full catalog; filter by genre at the top.
  • Explore — curated picks, genres, new uploads, and featured albums.
  • Trending — most-played public tracks.
  • Albums — browse collections; click an album to see the tracklist and play in order.
  • My Songs — your uploads (connect wallet). ♥ Playlist — songs you saved.
  • Click any artist name to open their channel. Tap 💰 Tip to send LCAI (90% to artist).
🌐 Public vs 🔒 Private

Public — your song appears in the main catalog for everyone to discover. Only upload music you created or have the rights to distribute publicly.

Private — only visible on your own channel. Good for personal listening, covers, or music you're not ready to share yet.

💰 Tip Artists

Love a track? Hit 💰 Tip Artist while listening. Tips go straight to the creator's wallet in LCAI — 90% to the artist, 10% to LightTunes to keep the relay running. Listening stays free; tipping is optional.

♥ Your Personal Playlist

Tap ♥ on any song — public or private — to add it to your personal playlist. Open ♥ Playlist in the nav and press play there — your saved songs will continue one after another in order. Browsing All Songs or genres plays one track at a time unless you turn on 🔁 Loop.

Your playlist is private — only you can see it, stored in your browser.

💡 Quick Tips
  • Add searchable tags on upload (e.g. chill, 90s, freestyle) — listeners can search #chill
  • Use 📤 Share to copy a direct link to any song
  • Press ▶ Play Album on an album page to hear tracks in order
  • Need albums or explicit help? Use the Help & Guide button (ℹ️) anytime
