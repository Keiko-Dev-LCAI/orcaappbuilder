    A LightJury verdict is the reasoned opinion of a five-member AI panel. It is advisory only. It is not a binding ruling, not a court judgment, and not a substitute for a licensed attorney. Do not rely on it as your sole basis for legal action. For anything serious, consult a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.

    Worldwide Use & Legal Basis

    LightJury is used worldwide. There are far too many local laws for any one service to apply them all. Verdicts reason from general common-law principles — the basis for law in most, but not all, U.S. states (Louisiana, for example, follows civil law and differs), and a useful reference in many countries. A verdict is a reasonable opinion based on those general principles and may not reflect the specific law where you live.
